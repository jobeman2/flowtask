# Product MVP & Milestone Plan

## Milestone Phases
1. **Phase 1 — Foundation (Current)**: Monorepo, Turborepo, NestJS modular monolith, Next.js Mini App, Grammy Bot, Prisma schema, auth, testing & CI.
2. **Phase 2 — Core Tasks**: Project boards, rich task CRUD, labels, comments, audit logs.
3. **Phase 3 — Telegram Bot Commands**: Full bidirectional sync with bot (`/task`, `/today`, `/tasks`, inline keyboard buttons).
4. **Phase 4 — Forward Message to Task**: Auto task extraction from forwarded Telegram messages with user confirmation.
5. **Phase 5 — Full Mini App UX**: Mobile-first Today, Inbox, Projects, Calendar, and Team views.
6. **Phase 6 — AI Assistant**: Natural language parsing, deadline extraction, Ethiopian context, message summarization.
7. **Phase 7 — Teams & Telegram Groups**: Group chat binding, team permissions, role-based assignments.
8. **Phase 8 — Ethiopian & Global Billing**: ETB pricing, subscription tiers, payment gateway adapters (Telebirr / Chapa / Stripe).
